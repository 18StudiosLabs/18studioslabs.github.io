THE DIFFERENCE

This is not another texture pack.

MOST "ANALOG" EFFECTS

Are just images placed over your work. A JPEG of grain, set to Overlay, hoping it reads. Static. Non-reactive. Same result every time.

GRAINSHIFT

Treats texture as a system — dither, halftone, levels, threshold, grain and controlled degradation, computed against your actual document.

WHY IT MATTERS

You get control over how dirty it becomes, not just a preset someone else decided on. Every effect stays editable after it lands.

HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. Under a minute.

01 · SELECT

Pick any layer. The panel shows exactly what it's targeting. Your original art is never modified.

02 · APPLY

Click a thumbnail. The effect lands above your layer with the right blend mode and opacity already set.

03 · ADJUST

Live, non-destructive controls for opacity, brightness, contrast, curve, levels and threshold. Stack as many effects as you want.
