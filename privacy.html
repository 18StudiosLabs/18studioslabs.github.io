Privacy

GRAINSHIFT  ·  18 STUDIOS LABS

The short version: the plugin collects nothing and never connects to the internet. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no account, and no tracking of any kind.

THE PLUGIN

GRAINSHIFT requests no network permission in its manifest. It cannot make outbound requests, because Adobe's plugin sandbox does not grant it the ability to. This is verifiable — the permission simply isn't declared.

The only data GRAINSHIFT writes is three small preference files, stored in its own sandboxed folder on your machine and never transmitted anywhere:

  • theme.txt — which interface theme you picked
  • customcolors.txt — your custom theme colors, if you set any
  • welcomed.txt — whether you've seen the welcome screen

That's the complete list. There are no exceptions. Your artwork, your documents, and your usage of the plugin never leave your computer.

THIS WEBSITE

If you enter your email address in the Lab Notes form, it's stored by our email provider purely to send you occasional updates about GRAINSHIFT and future 18 Studios Labs releases. We don't sell it, share it, or use it for anything else. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes you immediately.

Basic, anonymous visit counts may be recorded by our web host. No cookies are set for advertising or cross-site tracking.

DOWNLOADS

GRAINSHIFT is distributed through the Adobe Creative Cloud Marketplace. Adobe handles the download and any associated account data under Adobe's own privacy policy. We receive only aggregate, anonymous download counts — no personal information about who installed it.
